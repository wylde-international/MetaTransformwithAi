About the Programme

Meta’s First AI Training in Africa

The Meta Transform with AI Program is a strategic capacity-building initiative implemented by WYLDE International in partnership with Meta, designed to accelerate the adoption of artificial intelligence among Kenya’s small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). The programme equips entrepreneurs with practical knowledge and hands-on skills to leverage Meta’s AI-powered advertising solutions to improve marketing performance, optimise advertising investment, and drive sustainable business growth.

Through training delivered by internationally accredited Meta trainers, an engaging learning approach, and access to post-training resources, the programme strengthens entrepreneurs’ digital capabilities and supports the adoption of AI-enabled business practices.

Who Should Apply?

The programme is intended for SMEs that:

  • ✓ Are registered and revenue-generating businesses operating in Kenya.
  • ✓ Have 1–50 employees and clear growth ambitions.
  • ✓ Actively use Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp Business.
  • ✓ Have run paid advertising on Meta platforms within the last 12 months.
  • ✓ Will be represented by the business owner or a senior decision-maker.
